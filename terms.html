02

Statutory Execution Requirements (Section 63)

Under Section 63 of the Indian Succession Act 1925, generating or downloading a digital PDF Will document does not by itself make the Will legally effective. To attain full legal validity in India:

  • Physical Printout: The generated PDF must be printed on plain A4 paper (stamp paper is not legally mandatory under Indian law).
  • Testator Execution: The testator must physically sign or affix their thumb impression on each page in the presence of witnesses.
  • Two Independent Witnesses: At least two (2) adult witnesses must physically observe the testator signing and sign the document themselves in the testator's presence.
  • Beneficiary Disqualification: Under Section 67 of the Indian Succession Act 1925, named beneficiaries (or their spouses) must not act as attesting witnesses; doing so invalidates the bequest to that witness.
