Will Registration in Kolkata: Fees, SRO Offices & Probate (2026)

Everything you need to register a Will in Kolkata and West Bengal. Understand Calcutta High Court probate rules, WBREGOV portal fees under ₹400, SRO locations, and how to avoid ₹30,000 advocate fees.

Whether you own a heritage flat in South Kolkata, an apartment in Bidhannagar (Salt Lake), or commercial property in Central Kolkata, securing your family's future requires an understanding of West Bengal's unique succession laws.

Unlike inland states like Telangana or Karnataka, Kolkata is subject to historic statutory probate mandates under the Indian Succession Act 1925. A poorly drafted Will in Kolkata can trap your heirs in Calcutta High Court litigation for years.

The Calcutta High Court Probate Mandate: Crucial Legal Context

Section 213 & Section 57(a) ISA 1925: Under Indian law, Kolkata (along with Mumbai and Chennai) is one of the three former Presidency Towns where probate is compulsory for Wills executed by Hindus, Buddhists, Sikhs, and Jains if:
  • The Will was signed within the original civil jurisdiction of the Calcutta High Court; or
  • The Will bequeaths immovable properties situated within the municipal territory of Kolkata.

Because probate can only be granted to an executor named in the Will (Section 222), your Will must explicitly name a primary and alternate executor. SmartWill India's drafting engine automatically embeds these statutory clauses.

Government Registration Fees in Kolkata (West Bengal)

Many traditional advocates in Kolkata claim that registering a Will costs ₹20,000 to ₹40,000. In reality, the official government charges at West Bengal Sub-Registrar Offices are negligible:

  • Stamp Duty: ₹0 (Wills are 100% exempt from stamp duty under the Indian Stamp Act).
  • Government Registration Fee: Approx ₹100 – ₹200 (Article EE / Table of Fees).
  • Computer Scanning Fee: Approx ₹24 per page.
  • Total Official Government Cost: Under ₹400 to ₹500 for a standard 4-page Will.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Will registration mandatory in Kolkata?

No. Under Section 18 of the Registration Act 1908, Will registration is optional. An unregistered Will signed by the testator and two witnesses is legally valid. However, in Kolkata, registering your Will provides powerful proof of genuineness in Calcutta High Court probate proceedings.

What is the procedure for KMC (Kolkata Municipal Corporation) mutation?

Upon the testator's demise, the beneficiary submits an application for mutation (Form P-36) to the KMC Assessment Department along with the death certificate, registered Will (or Probate), and latest property tax receipt.

Can I bequeath a 999-year leasehold plot in Salt Lake or New Town?

Yes. Leasehold rights granted by the Urban Development Department (UDD) or HIDCO can be bequeathed in a Will. The beneficiary must later apply to the Department for mutation of tenancy rights.
