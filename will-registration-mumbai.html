The Mumbai Co-operative Housing Society (CHS) Problem

Over 80% of Mumbai's residential property is held under Co-operative Housing Societies (CHS). The single biggest mistake Mumbai flat owners make is believing that submitting a Society Nomination Form (Appendix 14) settles their flat's ownership.

The Legal Reality (Section 154B-13 MCS Act): The Supreme Court in Indrani Wahi v. Registrar of Co-operative Societies ruled that a society nominee is merely a temporary caretaker/trustee. The society transfers the share certificate to the nominee for administrative purposes, but the nominee has NO beneficial ownership. Without a legally executed Will, your legal heirs can drag the matter to the Bombay High Court for years.

Is Will Registration Mandatory in Mumbai?

No.

Under Section 18(e) of the Indian Registration Act 1908, Will registration is strictly optional across Maharashtra.

Plain Paper Validity: A Will drafted on ordinary A4 white paper and attested by two independent witnesses complies 100% with Section 63 of the Indian Succession Act 1925. SRO registration is an optional evidentiary safeguard, but not a prerequisite for legal validity.

Understanding the Bombay High Court Probate Requirement

Mumbai is one of the few jurisdictions in India where testamentary probate rules are specific:

  • Under Sections 57 and 213 of the Indian Succession Act 1925, if a Will is executed within the ordinary original civil jurisdiction of the High Court of Bombay (Mumbai City limits), or if immovable properties situated within Mumbai limits are bequeathed by a Hindu, Buddhist, Sikh, or Jain, obtaining a Probate from the Bombay High Court may be required if society management or banks demand it.

True Government Fees in Maharashtra (Under ₹500)

Official Maharashtra government registration fees are among the most economical in India:

  • Stamp Duty: ₹0 (Exempt under Article 64 of the Maharashtra Stamp Act).
  • Registration Fee: ₹100 statutory government fee.
  • Document Scanning Charges: Approximately ₹20 per page for electronic document storage on IGRMS.
  • Total Official Government Outlay: Under ₹300 to ₹500.

Step-by-Step Registration Procedure via Maharashtra IGRMS

  1. Generate Your Document: Draft your legally valid Will on SmartWill India for ₹299. Print two original copies on plain A4 paper.
  2. Visit Maharashtra IGRMS Portal: Visit igrmaharashtra.gov.in to verify the designated SRO based on your residence.
  3. Prepare Identification & Fitness Documents:
    • Testator original Aadhaar card + PAN card + 2 passport photos
    • Two independent adult witnesses with original Aadhaar cards (Witnesses must NOT be beneficiaries under Section 67!)
    • Medical fitness certificate from an MBBS/MD physician if the testator is 65+ years old
  4. Sub-Registrar Verification: Appear with your witnesses at the SRO. The Sub-Registrar interviews the testator, captures digital biometric thumbprints, and takes live photographs.
  5. Entry in Book 3: The document is officially sealed and permanently recorded in Book 3 (Register of Wills).

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I mention multiple flats in Mumbai and Pune in the same Will?

Yes. A single Will can cover all your properties across Mumbai, Pune, Thane, or anywhere in India, as well as movable assets like mutual funds, bank accounts, and jewellery.

2. What documents does the Housing Society require after demise?

The society typically requests: (1) Copy of the death certificate, (2) Copy of the registered or executed Will, (3) Application for transfer of shares in Form 20, and (4) An indemnity bond if probate is not yet completed.

3. Can an NRI who inherited a Mumbai flat write a Will?

Yes. Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) who inherit or purchase apartments in Mumbai can execute a Will specifically covering their Indian assets under the Indian Succession Act 1925.
