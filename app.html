💡 Important Legal Advisory for Execution (Indian Succession Act 1925)
1
Two Independent Witnesses: Under Section 67, never choose a beneficiary or their spouse as a witness (doing so voids their entire inheritance). Choose independent friends or neighbors.
2
Plain White Paper: Print your downloaded PDF on standard A4 paper. Stamp paper or notarization is NOT legally mandatory in India.
3
Safe Storage: Keep the physically signed original in a secure place (home safe or locker) and inform your trusted executor.
