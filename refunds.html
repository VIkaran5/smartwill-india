02

Digital Products & Non-Refundable Scope

SmartWill India provides automated, customized digital legal documents that are generated and delivered immediately upon payment authorization. In accordance with e-commerce standards for irreversible digital downloads:

  • Post-Download Finality: Due to the intangible and irreversible nature of digital legal document files, once a finalized Will PDF has been successfully generated and unlocked or downloaded to your device (as confirmed by our server audit verification logs), the service is deemed fully delivered and the purchase is strictly non-refundable.

All approved refunds (for technical failure or duplicate billing) are initiated directly through our payment partner, Cashfree Payments, and credited back to your original source of payment:
