Is Probate Mandatory for Wills in Delhi?

Huge Advantage for Delhi Residents: Unlike Mumbai and Chennai, Probate is NOT mandatory in Delhi under Sections 57 and 213 of the Indian Succession Act 1925. The Delhi High Court in landmark rulings (including Kanta Yadav v. Om Prakash Yadav) confirmed that a beneficiary can directly mutate DDA flats, MCD property tax records, and bank accounts using a valid Will without court probate!

Is Will Registration Mandatory in Delhi?

No, it is voluntary.

Under Section 18(e) of the Indian Registration Act 1908, registering a Will is optional in Delhi, Haryana (Gurgaon), and Uttar Pradesh (Noida). A Will executed on plain A4 white paper and attested by two adult witnesses fulfills every requirement of Section 63 of the Indian Succession Act 1925.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I include DDA freehold flats and builder floors in a Will?

Yes. A Will can cover all types of Delhi properties: DDA flats, cooperative group housing societies (CGHS), freehold builder floors, and agricultural land.

2. What happens to Gurgaon and Noida properties in a Delhi Will?

Under Indian law, a single Will validly executed anywhere in India covers properties located in Gurgaon (Haryana), Noida (UP), or anywhere across the country.

3. Can an unregistered plain paper Will be used for DDA mutation?

Yes. The Delhi Development Authority (DDA) accepts an unregistered Will for freehold mutation, accompanied by affidavits and no-objection certificates (NOC) from surviving legal heirs.
