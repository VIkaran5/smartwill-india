04

Legal Basis for Processing (Consent)

Under Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data is processed strictly on the basis of your free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent.

  • Affirmative Action: You provide explicit consent by checking the unticked DPDP consent box on Step 1 of the Will builder.
  • In-Memory Gating: No data is persisted to encrypted browser storage or cloud servers until this affirmative consent is given.
  • Right of Withdrawal: You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or emailing our Grievance Officer.

Your Rights under DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under Sections 11, 12, and 13 of the DPDP Act 2023, you hold full legal rights over your personal data:

Right to Access

You have the right to obtain a full summary of personal data held about you. Click "Download My Data (JSON)" in your dashboard or email us.

Right to Correction

You can review and modify any personal, asset, or family information at any time directly through the interactive Will wizard.

Right to Erasure

You have the "Right to be Forgotten". Click "Delete My Account & Data" to permanently purge your local and cloud records.

Right to Withdraw

You may withdraw your consent at any time without penalty. Future processing stops immediately upon withdrawal.

Right to Nominate

You have the right to nominate another individual who shall exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Right to Redressal

You have the right to have grievances addressed within 30 days, with the right to escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

09

Technical & Operational Security

  • Transport Encryption: 100% of website traffic is enforced over TLS 1.3 HTTPS with HSTS preloading.
  • Client-Side Scrambling: Unencrypted plain text is never committed to localStorage; Web Crypto AES-GCM 256-bit prevents casual inspection.
  • Least Privilege Cloud Access: Firestore Security Rules enforce strict UID matching, preventing any user from accessing another user's Will documents.
  • No Payment Storage: We adhere to PCI-DSS standards; card numbers and UPI PINs never touch SmartWill servers.
10

Minors Policy

Under Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, we do not knowingly process personal data of individuals under 18 years of age as account holders. To create a Will in India under the Indian Succession Act 1925, the testator must be a major of sound mind. If a minor beneficiary is named in a Will, an adult guardian must be designated.
