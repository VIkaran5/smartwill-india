Frequently Asked Questions

Is an online Will legally valid in India?
Yes! Under the Indian Succession Act 1925, any Will drafted clearly, printed on plain paper, signed by the testator, and witnessed by 2 independent adults is 100% legally valid. Registration or stamp paper is optional under Indian law.
What happens after I download the PDF?
Print the generated PDF, sign on every page in front of 2 witnesses (who also sign). Store it in a safe place or in your SmartWill vault.
Can I edit my Will later if I buy new property or assets?
Yes! You can log back into SmartWill anytime to update your assets or beneficiaries and download an updated Will for free.
Are my financial asset details secure?
Absolutely. We use modern AES-256 client and cloud encryption. Your private financial data is accessible only by you.
