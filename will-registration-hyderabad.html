Is Will Registration Mandatory in Hyderabad?

The short answer is No.

Under Section 18(e) of the Indian Registration Act, 1908, the registration of a testamentary document (Will) is purely optional throughout the state of Telangana.

The Legal Reality: A Will written or printed on plain A4 white paper and attested by two independent witnesses complies 100% with Section 63 of the Indian Succession Act, 1925. It is completely legally binding and admissible in civil courts for mutation and title transfer without stepping into a Sub-Registrar office.

Why Some Hyderabad Property Owners Choose SRO Registration

While registration is optional, registering your Will at a Hyderabad Sub-Registrar Office provides concrete advantages for high-value estates:

  • Protection against forgery allegations: The Sub-Registrar captures biometric thumbprints and a web-camera photograph of the testator, proving personal presence and soundness of mind.
  • Official government record in Book 3: The Registration Department archives a certified copy in Book 3 (Register of Wills). If the original paper is misplaced, heirs can obtain a certified copy from the department.
  • Seamless mutation on Dharani & GHMC records: Having a registered Will simplifies mutation of agricultural lands in Ranga Reddy / Medchal on the Telangana Dharani Portal and municipal assessment changes with GHMC.

True Government Fees at Hyderabad SROs (Under ₹500)

Do not let touts or middlemen mislead you about registration expenses. In the state of Telangana:

  • Stamp Duty: ₹0. Wills attract zero stamp duty under Section 18 of the Indian Stamp Act 1899.
  • Registration Fee: ₹100 to ₹200.
  • User / Scanning Charges: ₹100 to ₹250 (calculated per page scanned for electronic archiving).
  • Total Official Outlay: ₹300 to ₹500 maximum.

Step-by-Step Registration Procedure in Hyderabad

  1. Draft & Download Your Will: Use SmartWill India to generate your complete legal Will in 10 minutes. Print two copies on plain A4 paper.
  2. Book an Appointment: Visit the official Telangana Registration Department portal (registration.telangana.gov.in) or visit your designated local SRO directly.
  3. Gather Required Documents:
    • Testator original Aadhaar card + PAN card + 2 passport photos
    • Two independent witnesses with original Aadhaar cards (Note: Witnesses must NOT be beneficiaries under Section 67!)
    • Medical fitness certificate from an MBBS practitioner (recommended if testator is 65+ years old)
  4. Biometric Verification: At the SRO counter, the Sub-Registrar verifies mental capacity, takes biometric thumb impressions, and captures web-camera photographs.
  5. Signing & Endorsement: You and your two witnesses sign in the Sub-Registrar's presence. The SRO places the official seal and assigns a unique Document Registration Number in Book 3.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I register my agricultural land in Telangana using this Will?

Yes. A Will covers both movable assets (bank FDs, mutual funds, gold, demat shares) and immovable assets (flats, plots, agricultural land under Dharani). Post-demise, the beneficiaries present the Will along with the death certificate for revenue mutation.

2. What if my property is in Hyderabad but I live abroad (NRI)?

NRIs owning property in Hyderabad can execute a Will abroad by signing before an Indian Consular officer or Notary Public, or create their digital Will using SmartWill India and sign it during their visit to India in the presence of two witnesses.

3. Can an unregistered plain paper Will be used in Hyderabad courts?

Yes, absolutely. The Supreme Court has repeatedly upheld that registration is purely voluntary. An unregistered Will is 100% admissible for probate or title succession in civil courts across Telangana.

4. Can I modify or cancel a registered Will later?

Yes. You retain 100% ownership and control during your lifetime. You can revoke or amend your Will at any time by executing a new Will. Under Indian law, the latest dated valid Will always supersedes all previous Wills.
