Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the cheapest online will maker in India?

SmartWill India is the most cost-effective platform, charging a fixed ₹299 one-time fee with full ISA 1925 compliance. Competitors charge between ₹2,499 and ₹19,999.

Are online Wills legally valid in Indian courts?

Yes. Under Section 63 of the Indian Succession Act 1925, any Will printed on plain paper, signed by the testator of sound mind, and attested by two witnesses is 100% legally enforceable in all Indian civil courts.

Do online Wills need stamp paper?

No. Under the Indian Stamp Act 1899, Wills are completely exempt from stamp duty across India. Printing on plain A4 paper is 100% legal.
