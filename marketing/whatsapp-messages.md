## Tips for Sharing

1. Personalise — change [Name] to the actual person's name before sending
2. Send one at a time — personal messages convert far better than blasts
3. Best time — mornings (8-10am) or evenings (7-9pm)
4. Follow up once — if no response after 3 days, one polite follow-up is fine
5. WhatsApp Status — post the English message as your Status for passive reach
